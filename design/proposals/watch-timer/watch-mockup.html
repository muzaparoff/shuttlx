Countdown grows 56 → 66pt. One progress encoding, not three. Label column deleted; labels become 10pt captions above values.

  • The step rail replaces all three progress indicators. One tick per interval step: filled = done, glowing = current, tinted by step type. It answers "how far in?" and "how many left?" — the continuous bar answered neither. 6pt tall, full-bleed, one animation per step boundary instead of a per-second bar redraw.
  • Label column deleted. labelWidth = h * 0.20 ≈ 43pt of a 176pt screen. Labels move above values at 10pt; values gain ~25% width and stop needing minimumScaleFactor(0.4) — the fix that exists today because "2.15 km" was clipping.
  • Countdown 56 → 66pt (h * 0.26 → h * 0.31). It is the most decision-critical number during interval work and should dominate. Leading zero trimmed ("1:48"), matching what Mixtape already does.
  • Phase word is the second-loudest thing at 15pt/800 with wide tracking — readable mid-treadmill without parsing the countdown.
  • HR zone arc → 5 vertical ticks. The current HRZoneArc Canvas draws 5 arc strokes at 34×17pt; ascending bars read faster at that size and cost less to redraw. Colours unchanged (hrZone1…5).
  • Workout name removed. You chose it 40 seconds ago; it is the least useful pixel on the screen and it forced a 5th row. The step rail carries identity instead.
  • DIST/PACE pinned to the bottom. Fixed footer = stable layout across all states, so nothing jumps when the NEXT chip appears or the HR banner fires.
