How ShuttlX works

Every feature, step by step — from your first interval workout on Apple Watch to custom templates and training programs.

Start an interval workout on your watch

  1. Open ShuttlX on your Apple Watch and pick a workout — a built-in template like 5K Interval, or free run.
  2. Tap Start. The big timer shows the current interval; WORK and REST phases switch automatically with haptics.
  3. Watch your heart rate zone live — if your HR runs high, the "Ease off" banner tells you before you overdo it.
  4. Pause with the green button, finish with the red one. The workout saves to HealthKit and syncs to your iPhone automatically.

Build your own intervals on iPhone

  1. On iPhone, open the Programs tab and tap +.
  2. Set your work and rest durations, repeats, and warm-up/cool-down.
  3. Save — the template appears on your watch within moments, ready to start from the wrist or straight from a widget.

Built-in training programs

8 weeks  Couch to 5K

Go from zero to running 5K in 8 weeks. Three workouts per week with gradual progression.

4 weeks  HIIT Starter

High-intensity interval program. Three sessions per week building from 20s to 40s work intervals.

6 weeks  5K Improvement

Improve your 5K time with a mix of tempo runs, intervals, and easy runs — four sessions per week.

ongoing  Gym Heart Recovery

Two gym sessions per week. Your watch automatically monitors heart-rate recovery between sets — no scripted exercises.

History & analytics

  1. Every workout lands in the History tab with intervals, heart rate, pace and route.
  2. Analytics shows weekly volume, streaks and trends over time.
  3. All data lives in HealthKit and on your devices — see the privacy policy for the details.

Make it yours

  1. Pick a theme in Settings — from the clean glass look to the Walkman-style Mixtape deck. Your watch follows automatically.
  2. Add the Home Screen widget or a watch complication to start your favourite workout in one tap.
